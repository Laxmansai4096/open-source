# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT (MSA) - AMENDMENT NO. 1
**Contract Document ID:** CTR-2024-APEX-MSA-V2  
**Effective Date:** January 15, 2024 (Amended September 2026)  
**Governing Law:** State of Delaware, United States  
**Classification:** Enterprise Tier-1 Vendor Agreement  

---

### SECTION 1. PARTIES AND OPERATIONAL SCOPE
This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into by and between **Global Retail Logistics Corp** ("Client"), a Delaware corporation having its principal office at 500 Enterprise Way, Wilmington, DE, and **Apex Cloud Systems Inc.** ("Vendor"), a Delaware corporation having its principal office at 100 Tech Blvd, Wilmington, DE.

### SECTION 2. SERVICES AND INFRASTRUCTURE PROVISIONING
Vendor agrees to provide enterprise cloud infrastructure hosting, high-throughput database synchronization, and distributed telemetry computing services to Client and its designated operating subsidiaries.

### SECTION 3. SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENTS (SLA)
Vendor guarantees a monthly service availability of **99.99%** ("Commitment Level"). In the event availability drops below 99.90% in any calendar billing period, Client shall be entitled to a 25% service credit against the subsequent month's invoice.

### SECTION 4. SECURITY AND COMPLIANCE WARRANTIES
Vendor expressly warrants and represents that throughout the Term:
1. Vendor maintains active **SOC-2 Type II** certifications audited annually by an independent accredited CPA firm.
2. Vendor complies with ISO-27001 standards and maintains encrypted data transmission protocols (TLS 1.3 or higher).
3. Vendor shall immediately notify Client within **24 hours** of any confirmed or suspected cybersecurity intrusion, unauthorized data access, or ransomware event.

### SECTION 5. DATA PRIVACY AND TENANT ISOLATION
All Client data stored within Vendor infrastructure shall remain the sole proprietary property of Client. Vendor warrants strict logical and cryptographic tenant isolation, guaranteeing that no co-mingling of customer records shall occur.

### SECTION 6. TERM AND AUTO-RENEWAL PROVISIONS
The Initial Term of this Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue until **November 15, 2026**. 
This Agreement shall **automatically renew** for successive one (1) year periods unless either party provides written notice of termination at least **ninety (90) calendar days** prior to the expiration of the then-current term.

### SECTION 7. INVOICING AND PAYMENT TERMS
Client shall remit payment for undisputed invoices within forty-five (45) days of receipt (Net-45). Late payments shall accrue interest at the lesser of 1.0% per month or the maximum rate permitted under Delaware law.

### SECTION 8. ENTERPRISE VOLUME PRICING MATRIX
Pricing for computing and telemetry units shall be calculated according to the following tiered volume matrix:

| Volume Tier | Monthly Compute Units | Unit Price (USD) | Guaranteed SLA | Support Tier |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Tier 1 | 0 to 100,000 | $0.150 / unit | 99.90% | Standard 8x5 |
| Tier 2 | 100,001 to 500,000 | $0.125 / unit | 99.95% | Priority 24x7 |
| Tier 3 | 500,001 to 1,000,000 | $0.095 / unit | 99.99% | Dedicated TAM |
| Tier 4 (Enterprise) | Greater than 1,000,000 | $0.075 / unit | 99.99% | White-Glove VIP |

### SECTION 9. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY INDEMNIFICATION
Vendor shall defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Client against any third-party claims alleging that the software or infrastructure services infringe any valid patent, copyright, or trademark.

### SECTION 10. CONFIDENTIALITY AND NON-DISCLOSURE
Each party agrees to safeguard all proprietary and confidential information received from the disclosing party with at least the same degree of care it uses for its own confidential information, but in no event less than a reasonable standard of care.

### SECTION 11. AUDIT RIGHTS AND INSPECTION
Client shall have the right, upon fifteen (15) days written notice, to conduct an audit of Vendor's security posture, SOC-2 audit reports, and operational compliance.

### SECTION 12. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
EXCEPT FOR CLAIMS ARISING FROM GROSS NEGLIGENCE, WILLFUL MISCONDUCT, OR INDEMNIFICATION OBLIGATIONS UNDER SECTION 9, NEITHER PARTY SHALL BE LIABLE TO THE OTHER FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES.
**THE MAXIMUM AGGREGATE LIABILITY OF EITHER PARTY ARISING OUT OF OR RELATED TO THIS AGREEMENT SHALL BE STRICTLY LIMITED TO TEN MILLION DOLLARS ($10,000,000 USD).**

### SECTION 13. DISPUTE RESOLUTION AND ARBITRATION
Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be resolved by binding arbitration administered by the American Arbitration Association (AAA) in Wilmington, Delaware.

### SECTION 14. MISCELLANEOUS AND INTEGRATION
This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties with respect to the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior oral or written agreements, negotiations, and discussions.
